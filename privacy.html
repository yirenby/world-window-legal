Privacy Policy

隐私政策 · Effective 生效日期: 2026-10-28

1. YouTube API Services

World Window is a world map for a home ceiling projector. When you pick a place, it plays a YouTube video about that place. World Window uses YouTube API Services: the YouTube Data API (to build its video library) and the YouTube embedded player (to play videos). By using World Window you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

World Window 是给家用天花板投影的世界地图，选中一个地方就播放一个关于那里的 YouTube 视频。本应用使用 YouTube API 服务： 用 YouTube Data API 建视频库，用 YouTube 嵌入播放器播放视频。使用本应用即表示你同意受 YouTube 服务条款约束。Google 如何处理数据，见 Google 隐私政策。

2. What World Window collects and stores

World Window has no user accounts, no sign-in, no server of its own, no analytics and no advertising. It never asks for your Google or YouTube account and never accesses your YouTube data (it does not use OAuth). It does not collect, send or share any personal information.

On your own device, in your browser's local storage, it keeps three settings: the colour theme, the player mode, and whether you accepted this policy. They never leave your device and are not shared with anyone.

World Window 没有账号、不需要登录、没有自己的服务器，不做统计分析，不放广告。它不会要你的 Google 或 YouTube 账号， 也不读取你的 YouTube 数据（不使用 OAuth），不收集、不发送、不分享任何个人信息。 它只在你这台设备的浏览器本地存储里存三项设置：色系、播放方式、是否已同意本政策。这些不会离开你的设备，也不和任何人分享。

3. YouTube, Google and cookies

Videos play in YouTube's embedded player, which is provided by YouTube. When the player loads and plays, YouTube and Google may collect information about your device and your viewing, and may place, read or recognise cookies or similar technology on your device, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube may show advertisements in the player. World Window itself places no cookies and does not receive what YouTube collects.

视频在 YouTube 提供的嵌入播放器里播放。播放器加载和播放时，YouTube 和 Google 可能收集你的设备和观看信息， 并可能在你的设备上设置、读取或识别 cookie 或类似技术，详见 Google 隐私政策。YouTube 可能在播放器里显示广告。 World Window 本身不设置 cookie，也拿不到 YouTube 收集的数据。

4. The video library

The list of which video plays for which place is prepared in advance by the developer with the YouTube Data API, using only public information about public videos (video ID, title, channel name, duration, publish date, view count). It contains no information about you. Data from the API is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Videos that YouTube marks as made for kids are not used.

哪个地方播放哪个视频，是开发者事先用 YouTube Data API 整理的，只用公开视频的公开信息（视频编号、标题、频道名、时长、发布时间、播放量）， 不含任何关于你的信息。从 API 取得的数据在 30 天内刷新或删除。YouTube 标为儿童内容（Made for Kids）的视频不使用。

5. Deleting your data

To delete what World Window stores on your device, open Settings (key M) and choose 清除本机设置 (Clear settings on this device), or clear this site's data in your browser. Deleting it does not affect any data stored by YouTube; to manage that, use YouTube or your Google account. World Window never has access to your Google account, so there is nothing to revoke; you can always review app access at Google security settings.

要删除 World Window 在你设备上存的内容：打开设置（按 M），点"清除本机设置"；或者在浏览器里清除本站数据。 这不会影响 YouTube 存的任何数据，那部分请在 YouTube 或你的 Google 账号里管理。World Window 从不访问你的 Google 账号，没有需要撤销的授权； 你随时可以在 Google 安全设置查看各应用的访问权限。

6. Children

World Window does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

World Window 不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

本政策如有修改，上面的生效日期会更新，World Window 会请你重新同意。对隐私做法有疑问或投诉，请联系 13011298925@163.com。
